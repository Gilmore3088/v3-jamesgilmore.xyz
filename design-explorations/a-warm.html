Hi, I'm James.

I live in Seattle, work in fintech, run long distances, and like building things that didn't exist yesterday.

By day I help banks understand and grow their customer base. That means a lot of data, a lot of spreadsheets, and turning both into decisions people can act on.

The thing that really drives me is the moment an idea stops being abstract and becomes something real. With the tools we have now, that moment is closer than it has ever been, and I find that genuinely exciting.

This site is a home for the ideas, projects, and half-finished experiments along the way.

What I do

I help financial institutions understand and grow their customer base through data, analytics, and product strategy. Most days that means turning large, messy datasets into something a leadership team can act on.

Whenever something gets done by hand more than a few times, I try to automate it. That habit has saved a lot of hours and started more than a few side projects.

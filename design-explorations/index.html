Three ways your personal site could feel

No gimmicks this time. Each one is a plain, friendly homepage: a photo, a hello, what you do in normal words, what you're into, a few projects, some writing, and a way to reach you. They differ in warmth and how much room the photo gets. Open any tile to see the full page; a small A / B / C switcher in the corner flips between them.

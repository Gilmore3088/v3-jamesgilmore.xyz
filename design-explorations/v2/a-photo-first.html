I'm James. Seattle, by way of Florida. Husband, kayaker, builder of small tools, and a banker's data guy by trade.

I got into banking by way of a football stadium.

My first real job was in collegiate athletics at UCF. I helped build a student section brand called The Knightmare, worked on ticket sales, and built dashboards to track how the department was doing.

Then a few years consulting for university and pro sports: sponsorship valuations, negotiations, dashboards for executives who needed an answer by Friday. The data usually exists. What's missing is someone willing to make it legible.

Banking technology came next, and lately my own tools. The scale changed. The problem didn't.
